Here is the translation and breakdown of the Lusoga (Lukono dialect) phrase "ata maite okubona, ta maite okubala":

Literal Translation

"He who does not know how to see, does not know how to count."

Word-by-Word Breakdown

  • Ata maite: "He who does not know" (ata- is the relative negative prefix "he who does not", and maite is the perfective state of "to know").
  • Okubona: "To see," "to observe," or "to experience."
  • Ta maite: "He does not know" (ta- is the standard negative prefix "he does not").
  • Okubala: "To count," "to calculate," or "to evaluate."

The Deeper Meaning

Idiomatically, this proverb teaches that observation must precede evaluation. You cannot accurately quantify, judge, or make sense of a situation if you haven't first taken the time to truly look at and understand it.
